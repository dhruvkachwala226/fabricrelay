# User personas

These are research archetypes based on the brief. Goals and frustrations are hypotheses for interviews and observation.

| Persona | Access | Goals | Possible frustrations | Needs to test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform Super Admin | `SUPER_ADMIN` | Provision wholesalers, maintain business profiles, activate or suspend accounts and monitor activity. | Unclear onboarding state or difficulty tracing changes to accounts. | Business status, recent platform activity and a predictable suspension policy with explicit permissions. |
| Textile Wholesaler | `WHOLESALER_ADMIN` | Publish an accurate branded catalog, share it quickly and respond to viable inquiries. | Repeated questions, scattered photos and rates, availability drift and incomplete requests. | Fast entry and updates, fabric codes, color availability, price visibility controls and one inquiry queue. |
| Garment Manufacturer | Public buyer | Find fabric meeting production specs, evaluate a physical swatch and get a quote for a planned quantity. | Missing GSM or width, uncertain color or availability and repeated clarification before approval. | Searchable specs, named colors, MOQ in a clear unit and the ability to request a sample and quote. |
| Wholesale Fabric Buyer | Public buyer | Compare supply options and shortlist fabrics quickly for resale or procurement. | Stale forwards, ambiguous prices and unclear stocking status. | A mobile catalog link, search by quality or design code, availability, and a short inquiry form. |

There are two authenticated roles. The garment manufacturer and wholesale fabric buyer are unauthenticated personas using the same public buyer workflow. Use [the research guide](market-validation.md) to check how well these archetypes represent actual tasks.
