# Problem statement

Surat textile wholesalers who sell through relationships may spend time finding and resending fabric photos, specifications, colorways, rates and availability. Buyers may have to ask several questions before they can shortlist a fabric or request a physical swatch or quotation. The size and frequency of these problems remain unknown.

## Evidence status

| Status | What we know |
| --- | --- |
| Project brief | The owner supplied the workflow hypothesis, proposed roles and a synthetic Pure Modal Satin example. |
| Publicly observed | WhatsApp Business provides shareable catalogs, item links and carts that bundle selections in a message. TradeIndia displays Surat modal-satin supplier listings with specification, MOQ and quote prompts on some entries. | Unverified locally | Channel prevalence, catalog freshness, repeated questions, inquiry loss, adoption, and willingness to maintain an additional catalog. |

## Hypotheses and root causes

| Reported pain to test | Possible cause | Competing explanation and test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated customer messages | Answers live in separate chats, photos or notebooks and lack a stable fabric reference. | Staff may prefer chat to qualify buyers and build trust. Observe ten recent inquiries and count messages that ask for facts already available. |
| Outdated catalogs | Exported PDFs and forwarded photos persist after price or stock updates. | The internal stock sheet or supplier feed may already be stale. Trace a recent change from owner to buyer and identify where the delay began. |
| Inconsistent rates | Price is shared without its unit, MOQ, date or applicable deal terms. | Buyer-specific negotiation may explain differences. Ask staff to explain two quotes on the same design and compare quantities and terms. |
| Difficult discovery | Fabric names, design codes, GSM, width and colors may lack consistent labels or searchable fields. | Buyers may already know a trusted supplier’s codes, or require a physical swatch for hand feel. Observe both known-code and open-ended sourcing tasks. |
| Unorganized inquiries | Requests arrive through several channels without a fabric/color reference, quantity, owner or status. | Staff may already manage follow-up with labels or a spreadsheet. Trace recent sample and quote cases to completion and look for re-entry or missed follow-ups. |

A shareable branded catalog might improve discovery and capture request context. The workflow depends on a wholesaler keeping the data current and buyers trusting the listing enough to act. A well-maintained WhatsApp catalog, linked spreadsheet or marketplace could already meet those needs for some businesses.
