# Proposed success metrics

The following targets are for a directional pilot. Baselines and measured FabricRelay results are pending for every metric.

| Metric | Proposed target | How to measure | Measured result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Time to create a listing | Median ≤ 3 minutes | Time from opening a new draft to seeing it published, with one photo and the fixture data ready. Use at least five wholesaler attempts and log unsuccessful attempts separately. | Pending |
| Time to share a catalog | Median ≤ 20 seconds | Start on the catalog dashboard. Stop when the correct published URL is pasted into a draft message. Measure at least five attempts. | Pending |
| Buyer fabric discovery | At least 80% correct unaided within 45 seconds | Use ten timed tasks across at least five buyers, split between known design codes and criteria-based searches. Count wrong selections, timeouts and assistance as failures. | Pending |
| Inquiry submission completion | At least 90% within 90 seconds | Measure ten attempts, five sample and five quotation, from form open to persisted request and confirmation. Include retries and abandonments in the denominator. | Pending |
| Mobile usability | At least 90% unaided core tasks completed | Observe 20 tasks across at least five users on 360–390 px viewport phones, including search, opening details and each inquiry form. Record blocking issues separately and target zero. | Pending |
| Repetitive communication | At least 30% lower median count per inquiry | For at least three wholesalers, compare ten similar inquiries per business before and during the pilot. Classify repeated spec, rate and availability prompts with the same rubric and calculate the percent change against the pre-pilot median. If the baseline median is zero, report raw counts instead. | Pending |

Record participant codes, task variants, device, sample size, failures and timing evidence without identifying buyers. Report pilot results with the denominator and known sampling limitations. See the [validation plan](market-validation.md).
