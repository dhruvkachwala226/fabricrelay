# Alternatives and competitive landscape

## Context

The comparison considers how a Surat wholesaler can send current product information to an existing buyer and follow through on sample and quotation requests. Public product documentation supports capability observations. Workflow fit and adoption in Surat remain preliminary.

## FabricRelay baseline

The proposed offer combines a wholesaler-branded mobile catalog, textile-specific fields, optional public pricing and structured sample and quotation intake in one tenant-scoped workflow. WhatsApp remains a distribution and follow-up channel. See the [MVP scope](mvp-scope.md).

## Potential differentiation

Test the complete handoff from a shared link to the correct design code and color, then to a sample or wholesale quote request that arrives in the wholesaler’s queue with usable context. Public search and textile details could reduce clarification, while the seller retains control of branding, visibility and communication. A PDF or spreadsheet can remain an internal or offline companion.

## Confidence and gaps

The sources describe or show capabilities at the time of desk research. The comparison does not establish feature absence, operational superiority, demand or exclusivity. No account, CRM or transcript evidence was needed for this product-category comparison. Direct task observation with Surat wholesalers and buyers can establish adoption, relative effort and the smallest combination that matters. See the [research protocol](market-validation.md).
