# Risks and assumptions

Initial ratings are qualitative. The fieldwork plan defines how to gather evidence and record counterexamples.

| ID | Risk or assumption | Impact | Evidence or response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Wholesalers have enough repeat work to justify another tool. | High | Compare recent inquiry histories and task timings against their best current setup. Reconsider if catalog or chat labels already handle the main pain. |
| R-02 | Someone can keep rate and availability current. | High | Trace a real change and identify its source and owner. Use manual availability plus clear update responsibility in the pilot. |
| R-03 | Public price visibility fits negotiated trade. | High | Ask which rates are safe to forward and why. Support quote-only listings and clearly identify indicative public rates. |
| R-04 | Physical swatches remain important and photos may misrepresent color or feel. | High | Observe what buyers check before approving fabric. Use named colorways, actual photographs and the sample request workflow. |
| R-05 | MOQ and fabric terms vary across businesses. | Medium | Compare how shops describe panna, lumps and meters and whether standard fields support their most common cases. |
| R-06 | Guest intake attracts spam or exposes contact data. | High | Bound input and throttle submissions, keep contact details private, and verify tenant scoping across queue access. |
| R-07 | Solo development misses the one-month window. | High | Prioritize the complete Must Have loop and defer nonessential filters and imports when schedule pressure appears. |
| R-08 | Suspending one business or updating its catalog leaks stale or wrong-tenant data. | High | Test suspension, publication state and cross-tenant checks with two distinct businesses, including direct URLs. |
| R-09 | Buyers may prefer WhatsApp or abandon a slow mobile page. | High | Use mobile task observation and time the same journey in the current channel. Keep catalog links shareable from the buyer's existing conversation. |

Open questions include willingness to maintain a catalog, peak rate of stock changes, preferred languages, safe price-disclosure patterns, and whether buyers choose sample and quote paths unaided. See the [interview guide](market-validation.md) and [proposed pilot measures](success-metrics.md).
