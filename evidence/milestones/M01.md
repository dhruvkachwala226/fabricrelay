## Engineering decisions

- Treated messaging friction, catalog drift, inconsistent rates, discovery and request handoffs as hypotheses. Each has a competing explanation and a field test. See [problem statement](../../docs/01-understand/problem-statement.md).
- Proposed one catalog per active business, manual onboarding, two authenticated roles, and guest discovery and request intake. Adopted a stable copyable link for distribution, optional indicative rates, manually maintained color availability, and a simple tenant-scoped inquiry queue. Staff would follow up through their existing channels. See [MVP scope](../../docs/01-understand/mvp-scope.md).
- Compared WhatsApp Business catalogs, PDFs, traditional e-commerce, manual spreadsheets and marketplaces. Used sources for documented features and treated local workflow fit and potential differentiation as preliminary. See [competitor analysis](../../docs/01-understand/competitor-analysis.md).
- Proposed separate sample and quotation forms. Samples need no shipping address at submission. Quotes capture planned meters and flag quantities below a displayed MOQ when a meter equivalent is available. See [requirements](../../docs/01-understand/requirements.md).
