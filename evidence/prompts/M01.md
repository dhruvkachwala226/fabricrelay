## Problem Hypothesis

Textile wholesalers frequently share fabric
photographs, specifications, colorways,
wholesale rates and availability through
WhatsApp and physical catalogs.

This may result in:

- Repetitive customer communication.
- Outdated fabric catalogs.
- Inconsistent pricing information.
- Difficulty discovering available fabrics.
- Unorganized sample and quotation requests.

## Proposed Solution

FabricRelay is a multi-tenant B2B textile catalog
and wholesale inquiry management platform.
Wholesalers can digitize their fabric collections
and publish branded, shareable catalog links.

## User Roles

1. SUPER_ADMIN

- Manage wholesaler accounts.
- Manage business profiles.
- Activate or suspend businesses.
- Monitor platform activity.

2. WHOLESALER_ADMIN

- Manage business branding.
- Upload fabric photographs.
- Manage textile specifications.
- Manage colorways, prices and availability.
- Publish branded catalogs.
- Manage sample and quotation requests.

3. PUBLIC BUYER (Unauthenticated visitor)

- Browse digital fabric catalogs.
- Search and filter fabrics.
- View specifications and colorways.
- Request physical samples.
- Request wholesale quotations.

There are only two authenticated roles.
